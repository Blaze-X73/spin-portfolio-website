Origins

The Spin is a technique that originates from the Neapolitan Executioners of Italy. It was developed to grant those given the death penalty a painless death by numbing their bodies before executing them. It is not a brawler's trick, rather a precise skill passed down from generation to generation of the Zeppeli family.
Though the exact location of the Zeppeli family of doctors and executioners is not known, their execution grounds are said to be located in the Piazza del Mercato in Sienna, Italy.

Nature of the Spin

The spin is an art taught and passed down among the first sons of the Zeppeli family of doctors and executioners.
It is not some mystical power rather a scientific discipline gotten from the Golden Ratio (about 1 : 1.618), a naturally occurring mathematical proportion that generates self-sustaining, infinite rotational energy.
